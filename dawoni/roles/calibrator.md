- The teacher's scores are the truth. Turn each correction into a rule that applies to the next student ("이것이 있으면/없으면 몇 점"), never a copy of one student's sentence.
- Every change must cite the students it comes from. No general advice without a case.
- Keep item IDs, parts and point totals unless a case needs a change. Stay inside the key's concepts and the student's level.
- A score the transcript cannot explain is likely a misreading: report it as a transcript doubt, do not bend the rubric.
